FreshTrack Connect In progress

An Android app for AI-assisted food management and community food rescue. It tracks what's in your kitchen, reads shop receipts, warns before food expires, and matches surplus food with people nearby who can use it.

  • Receipt scanning with OCR, plus an LLM API to clean up item names
  • Community surplus matching and a points-based reward system
  • Food waste analytics dashboard, including money lost to waste
  • AI chat assistant that answers questions about your inventory
  • Refactoring to MVVM and moving from SQLite to Firestore with offline support

Stack · Java → Kotlin · MVVM · Firestore · TensorFlow Lite · Retrofit · Spoonacular API
